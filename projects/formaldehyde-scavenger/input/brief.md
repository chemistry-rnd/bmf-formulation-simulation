# Project brief

Develop a composition for treatment of fresh open cuts of particleboard/MDF made at the customer's premises during furniture installation.

The treatment is applied by an ordinary installer after a new cut is made. Fastener holes are secondary.

Core product requirements:
- reduce formaldehyde release from the treated exposed surface over the important first 1–3 months;
- fast drying compatible with installation workflow;
- no significant film thickness or unacceptable board swelling;
- no strong objectionable odor;
- safe for installer/occupants under intended use, subject to appropriate evidence;
- inexpensive per treated area/order;
- compatible with laminate, edge systems, fittings, silicone/sealants and relevant installation materials;
- no noticeable marks on visible finished surfaces.

Discovery emphasis:
- compare many plausible formulations;
- determine key component concentration windows, ratios and interaction effects;
- use measurement/experiment logic rather than selecting one guessed formula.

Future optional extension: a professional controlled-dose applicator. It is not required for the initial chemistry concept.
