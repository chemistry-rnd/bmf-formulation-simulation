# Project Requirements — Formaldehyde Scavenger

## Application scenario
- Primary target: fresh particleboard/MDF cuts made at the customer's premises during furniture installation.
- Holes/fastener openings are secondary.
- Do not describe the core use case as factory-only treatment.

## Document emphasis
- Explain the formulation-selection logic and the measurable criteria.
- Give the formaldehyde-reduction validation protocol a dedicated section.
- Treat odor as both instrumental emissions and sensory perception; do not equate “no odor” with TVOC alone.
- Include compatibility and dimensional-effect testing.
- Include patent landscape because the project has substantial chemistry/technology depth.
- Patent review should distinguish relevant world and Russian landscape where useful and summarize the most relevant patents concisely.
- Include competitors/market analogues and explain technical differentiation.
- Application-device R&D is a separate optional section, not the core technology.

## Formulation discovery
- Do not present one arbitrary recipe as the entire search strategy.
- Explain generation/comparison of multiple admissible formulation variants.
- Explicitly analyze component ratios, concentration windows and synergistic/antagonistic interactions.


## Technology card and patent appendix (project-specific)
- Describe the proposed treatment as a concrete set of functional material components and human application operations, not only an abstract discovery pipeline.
- Include a compact technological/process card table with operation, material/functional component, operator action, process control and expected intermediate result.
- Keep this card in the substantive technology/process section, not as a redundant conclusion.
- Refer to **Приложение № 7 — Проект патентной заявки: состав и способ обработки открытых срезов древесных плит** for detailed formulation variants, chemical identities, quantitative composition ranges, process conditions and claims.
- The appendix is a separate future document; do not assert it has already been drafted, filed or patented. The main description may reference it as the designated specification location.


## R004 — Quantitative technology embodiment
- Specify named candidate chemical ingredients and formulation ranges with a complete mass balance. Treat these as proposed engineering parameters, not proven results.
- Describe both preparation/manufacturing and installer application, including equipment, dose in g/m², number of coats, drying conditions and measurable acceptance criteria.
- Compare our design with named products using one vertical matrix: many metric rows and a few product columns. Put links to official product pages in column headings. Keep patent links in the patent section.
- Patent application drafting is not required now; Appendix 7 remains a reserved technical appendix.
